Put your memory photos in this folder, named:

  1.jpg  2.jpg  3.jpg  4.jpg  5.jpg  6.jpg

They appear as floating polaroids "in her mind" after her eyes close.
To change the captions or use different file names, edit CONFIG.memories
near the top of the <script> in index.html.

HER PHOTO (makes the "Found You" scene look real)
- Put her photo next to index.html (NOT in this folder), named:  girl.jpg
- Optional: a second photo with her eyes closed, named:  girl-closed.jpg
  (it cross-fades in when her eyes close; without it, the screen closes like eyelids)
- The arrow lands at CONFIG.girl.heart = [0.5, 0.8]  -> 50% across, 80% down the photo.
  Change those two numbers if her heart is somewhere else in the picture.
- Without girl.jpg, a cinematic backlit silhouette by a night window is shown.

THE WISH
- The love letter typed out at the end is CONFIG.wish in index.html
  (eyebrow, title, lines, sign). Edit the text freely.

Tips
- Any size works; photos are centre-cropped to fit.
- Keep each photo under ~1 MB so the page stays fast.
- Until a memory photo exists, a soft placeholder card with an emoji is shown.
